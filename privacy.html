Summary

Skyshot Golf, made by Jacob Grigg, doesn't ask for your name, email or any account. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your scores, stars, unlocked items and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. To confirm and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous ID created by the app, your purchase history, and basic device information such as device model, OS version, app version and App Store country. This information is used only to unlock what you bought, is not linked to your identity, and is not used for advertising or tracking.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
