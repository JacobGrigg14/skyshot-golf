Contact

Questions, bugs or feedback? Email jacob@dgrigg.com. Include your device model and the world and hole if something went wrong.

Restoring purchases

Bought Ice Caves or Volcano and they're locked on a new or reset device? Open Play to reach the Worlds screen and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought them with.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Saved progress

Your scores, stars and unlocked balls and trails are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes this progress; paid worlds can always be restored.
